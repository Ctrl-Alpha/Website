## Meeting Overview

This was our first official meeting to discuss the formation of Ctrl Alpha, the Quantitative Finance Club at IIIT Hyderabad.

## Key Discussions

### Club Vision
We discussed the vision for the club and agreed on the following objectives:
- Promote interest in quantitative finance among students
- Organize regular workshops and talks
- Facilitate networking with industry professionals
- Participate in trading competitions

### Initial Activities
Proposed activities for the first semester:
- Introduction to Quantitative Finance workshop
- Guest lecture series
- Study groups on relevant topics
- Coding challenges focused on financial algorithms

### Organization Structure
We discussed potential roles and responsibilities:
- President: Overall coordination and strategy
- Technical Lead: Managing technical workshops and projects
- Events Coordinator: Organizing events and external collaborations
- Communications Lead: Managing social media and outreach

## Decisions Made

1. Officially register the club with the institute
2. Create a website using GitHub Pages
3. Set up communication channels (Discord/Slack)
4. Schedule first workshop for mid-February

## Next Steps

The action items listed above will be completed before our next meeting scheduled for January 20th.
