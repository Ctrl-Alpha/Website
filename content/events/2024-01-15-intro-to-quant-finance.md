## About the Event

Join us for an exciting introduction to the world of quantitative finance! This workshop is designed for students who are curious about how mathematics and computer science intersect with finance.

## Topics Covered

- What is Quantitative Finance?
- Algorithmic Trading Basics
- Financial Markets Overview
- Tools and Technologies
- Career Opportunities

## Who Should Attend

This event is perfect for students from all backgrounds - whether you're from CSE, Mathematics, Economics, or any other field. No prior knowledge of finance is required!
